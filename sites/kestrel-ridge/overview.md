# Kestrel Ridge

The ridge path above the reservoir, worked for raptors on a north-westerly. Kestrels
hover over the rough grass on the eastern shoulder and a pair of hobbies has hunted the
ridge every summer since 2019.

The viewpoint is exposed; there is no cover for a group and the wind is relentless.
