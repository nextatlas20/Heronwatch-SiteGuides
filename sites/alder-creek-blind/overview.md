# Alder Creek Blind

A timber hide on the west bank of Alder Creek, screened by alder scrub. The hide is
small — four people at most — and is the quietest place on the reserve in early
morning.

Water levels in the creek are managed, so the muddy margin in front of the hide moves
from week to week.
