# Gull Rock Landing

A shingle spit reached from the coast road. The landing is the society's best winter
gull roost: the birds come in an hour before dusk and the light comes from behind the
observer.

Bring a scope if you have one; the roost sits about 120 m offshore and binoculars alone
leave most of the ringed birds unresolved.
