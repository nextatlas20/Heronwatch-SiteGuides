# Osprey Narrows

The narrows between the sandbank and the far shore, watched from the shingle bank on the
south side. Ospreys fish the channel through the autumn passage and the bank is the only
reliable place to see them at eye level.

The tide runs hard through the narrows; do not attempt the sandbank crossing.
