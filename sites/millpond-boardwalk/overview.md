# Millpond Boardwalk

A level boardwalk on the northern edge of the old millpond, screened by reeds. Kingfishers
work the pond edge through the winter and the reed bed holds a small population of
bearded tits.

The boardwalk is the society's most accessible site and is used for the beginner walks.
