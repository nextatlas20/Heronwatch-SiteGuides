# Saltmarsh Point

A low wooden platform looking over the tidal creek where it opens into the bay. Best
worked on a rising tide in the two hours before high water, when the waders come in to
feed along the mud edge.

The platform takes about twenty people comfortably. There is no shelter, so check the
forecast before committing a group.
